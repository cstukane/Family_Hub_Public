Perfect Pasta Carbonara

Prep: 10 min | Cook: 15 min

Ingredients:

  • 400g spaghetti
  • 200g pancetta or bacon
  • 4 large eggs
  • 100g Pecorino Romano cheese, grated
  • Freshly ground black pepper

Steps:

  1. Bring a large pot of salted water to boil. Add spaghetti and cook until al dente.
  2. Fry pancetta in a large pan until crispy. Remove from heat.
  3. Whisk eggs and cheese together. When pasta is done, add to pan with pancetta, then add egg mixture.
  4. Stir quickly to coat pasta without scrambling eggs. Add pasta water if needed.
  5. Season with black pepper and serve immediately.
